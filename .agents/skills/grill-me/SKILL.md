# Grill Me

- Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this topic until we reach a shared understanding.
- Walk down each branch of the design tree.
  - For each question, provide your recommended answer and thoughts.
- Ask questions one topic at a time.
- Explore the codebase to feed on existing patterns, but don't assume they should apply.
